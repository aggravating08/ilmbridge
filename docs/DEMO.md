# Three-minute live demo

## Before judging
Run `./start-local.sh` on the development Mac, or follow the standard Ollama setup in the README. Open http://127.0.0.1:8765. Verify the local AI banner says connected. Model setup requires a download; complete it before losing internet.

## 0:00 - Problem
"Students already have notes. What they often need is a way to turn those notes into understanding and recall, without buying another subscription or uploading their material. IlmBridge is a local study companion."

## 0:25 - Understand
Show the sample biology notes. Ask: "How do light-dependent reactions support the Calvin cycle?" Choose Local AI explanation and English. Click Explore question. Explain that Qwen2.5 0.5B is an Apache-2.0 model running through local Ollama. Open a source panel and compare the answer to the original passage. Citations are inspectable references, not proof of correctness.

## 1:15 - Recall
Open Revision cards, answer the missing term aloud, and flip a card. Open Test yourself. Enter Photosynthesis in the first gap and leave another blank. Check the score. Show the source sentence accompanying the feedback.

## 1:50 - Take it away
Export the Markdown study pack. Explain that notes are held in memory and no account or app telemetry is required. Initial model download needs internet; subsequent local operation does not.

## 2:15 - Impact and contribution
"We target SDG 4 through self-directed learning, and SDG 10 through free code and selectable explanation languages. These are intended benefits; we have not measured educational outcomes."

Name the original work: lexical retrieval, traceable source tools, a unified revision workflow, interface and validation. Disclose Codex assistance. Do not claim Kiro usage.

## 2:45 - Boundaries
"This is a functional prototype. The small model can be wrong, lexical retrieval misses paraphrases, and Urdu quality needs evaluation. Next we would add teacher-reviewed tests and semantic retrieval."

## If local AI is unavailable
Switch to Source search. Say clearly that it retrieves exact text without AI generation. Cards, quizzes, and export remain functional. Do not describe source-only mode as a live model demonstration.
